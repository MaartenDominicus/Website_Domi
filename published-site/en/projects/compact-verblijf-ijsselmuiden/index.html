Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Use the height as well as the floor

New build from the foundations on screw piles. In a small retreat, every decision needs to serve its use. Here, a sleeping platform sits above a second sleeping space, with storage alongside. Using height leaves more room for living below. A ladder provides access to the platform; for your own plan, consider who will use it and whether that access is practical and accessible enough.

02

Everyday facilities within reach

The kitchen along one wall keeps the layout compact, while the bathroom includes a shower with a glass screen, vanity and illuminated mirror. Everyday facilities are part of the retreat rather than an afterthought. With limited room, easy access matters, along with ensuring that doors, furniture and circulation routes do not obstruct one another.

03

Plan around usable internal space

The project includes the timber frame, cladding, terrace and interior finish. Together these determine how much usable space remains. For a similar plan, consider more than external dimensions: wall and floor build-up take up space too. Using actual internal dimensions helps prevent a workable idea on paper becoming too cramped when built.
