Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Everyday functions in their place

A bathroom needs to do more than bring attractive materials together. The positions of the shower, toilet and basin determine how easily you can move through it. In this bathroom the functions are arranged alongside one another, with a glass screen enclosing the shower area.

02

Room for daily essentials

The basin cabinet provides a place to keep everyday belongings together. This helps keep the basin clear and makes the room easier to use. For your own renovation, decide which items need to be close at hand and how much storage they need before ordering the sanitaryware.

03

Choosing finish and services together

Light tiles and walls provide a calm background for the wood-toned cabinet. Less visible choices matter too: the drain position, accessible connections and ventilation. Consider these alongside the tile layout so the appearance and everyday use of the room work together.
