Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Your taste and your daily routine

A bathroom should suit your taste and work on an ordinary morning. The timber vanity with two basins provides two places to wash here. A patterned floor, green wall tiles and decorative border give the room character. For your own choices, space around the vanity matters at least as much as the number of basins.

02

Plan tiles and connections together

The shower with its glass screen and copper-coloured fittings relates to the rest of the room. A continuous tile border visually connects the elements, but tile layouts also need to fit around taps, sockets and the vanity. Agreeing those positions early lets use and appearance guide the work together, instead of having to work around each other later.

03

Care beneath the tiled finish

The renovation also includes pipework and preparation around the toilet and basin. A bathroom involves more than visible finishes: wet areas need attention to the waterproof build-up beneath the tiles, since tiles and grout alone are not sufficient. Discuss the substrate and connections as well as colours and fittings before finishing starts.
