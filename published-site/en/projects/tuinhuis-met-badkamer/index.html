Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Starting with everyday use

An entrance of its own makes a garden building more independent from the main house in everyday use. A wide window brings daylight inside and provides a garden view. For a similar project, we first discuss the purpose of the space: working, relaxing or a combination of activities. This determines how much open space is needed and where a bathroom will fit without getting in the way.

02

Bathroom facilities in a small space

The bathroom brings a shower, vanity and toilet together in a small footprint. This requires careful planning: a door or shower enclosure must open without leaving you squeezed between fixtures. For your project, we discuss how the room will be used, the size of the vanity and access to connections. A compact bathroom should remain comfortable to use and straightforward to clean.

03

Planning connections and finishes together

Light wall finishes and a separate shower enclosure keep the small room clear and easy to understand. A bathroom in a garden building requires water supply, drainage and ventilation to be considered from the start. The options depend on the existing situation and the distance to connection points. We look at these together before fixing the layout. This project combines a timber garden building with finished bathroom facilities, while keeping the wider use of the space in mind.
