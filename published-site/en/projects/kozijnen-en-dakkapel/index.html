Project story

A closer look at the project

01

A well-finished upstairs room

An upstairs room is more pleasant to use when windows are accessible and the surrounding finish sits calmly alongside the rest of the space. Here the frames of an existing dormer can be viewed from inside and outside, showing how the finish meets the room and exterior.

02

Considering what to retain and repair

The condition of existing timber is an important starting point for frame work. A new coat of paint and local repairs are different tasks. Discuss which parts remain sound and which need investigation or repair, so the proposed approach reflects the actual condition.

03

Beyond the visible surfaces

Junctions with the facade and surrounding roof parts deserve attention alongside the visible surfaces. Plan access, preparation and painting together. This makes it clear which parts of the room need to remain accessible and which tasks require preparation from outside.
