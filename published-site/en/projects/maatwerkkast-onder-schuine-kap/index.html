Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Making an awkward corner useful

An attic wall will not always suit a standard cupboard. Taking the roof shape into account creates storage where the space might otherwise be difficult to use. Here the cupboard front extends beneath the roof, reducing the need for separate storage boxes around the room.

02

Storage based on your belongings

What goes behind the doors determines how useful a cupboard feels day to day. Shelves in this cupboard provide space to organise belongings at different heights. For your own storage, start with the contents: which items need to be easy to reach, and which can sit higher up?

03

Joinery that fits the room

Bespoke storage also needs space in front of it. Doors should open without obstructing the route through the room, while the floor and roof junctions need consideration. Plan the layout and finish together so the cupboard suits the room as well as the available dimensions.
