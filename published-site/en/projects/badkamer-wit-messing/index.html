Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Room for the morning routine

This project went beyond the bathroom: we renovated the whole house. The bathroom is one part of that home renovation. Two basins make it easier to use the room together. Drawers in the wide vanity keep everyday items close to hand.

02

A shower that suits everyday use

The glass screen keeps the shower visually connected to the room. A rain shower and hand shower offer different ways to use it. In a similar renovation, tap position, floor drainage and the floor slope need to be coordinated beforehand. Waterproof connections beneath the tiles are part of the technical preparation.

03

Materials and lighting working together

Vertical white tiles keep the walls calm. Brown bands connect the finishes around the shower and basins, while timber and brass-coloured taps add warmth. Mirror lighting and ceiling spots provide light in different places. Choosing tiling, sanitary fittings and lighting together helps make a bathroom both pleasant to look at and practical to use.
