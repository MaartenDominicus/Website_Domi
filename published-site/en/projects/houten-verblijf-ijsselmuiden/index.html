Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Connecting indoors and outdoors

New build from the foundations on screw piles. A retreat becomes comfortable when indoor and outdoor spaces connect naturally. In IJsselmuiden, the terrace beside the glazed front extends the living space outside. Timber cladding and decking bring the exterior together. For your own retreat, start with where you want to sit, which side offers a view and how you will move between indoors and outdoors.

02

Cooking and spending time together

Inside, kitchen, dining and seating areas share an open space. This lets you cook while staying connected with others in the retreat. The green kitchen, timber worktop and exposed beams give this shared area character. For a similar layout, consider circulation and connection points so furniture and everyday use do not get in each other’s way.

03

Include facilities from the start

A separate bedroom and bathroom keep sleeping and washing apart from the shared living area. The bathroom combines a freestanding bath, timber basin top and patterned floor. The project brings timber construction and varied interior finishes together. For your own plan, include these facilities when arranging the layout, rather than after the exterior has been fixed.

From build to retreat

Luka’s Hut

This timber retreat on the Ganzendiep is Luka’s Hut from Fairytale Cabins. We built the house with room to cook and spend time together, plus a separate bedroom and bathroom. Its connection to the terrace brings outdoor living into the stay.

The official project page shows the retreat in use. Rental and guest information belong to Fairytale Cabins.
