Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Start with a usable layout

A bathroom is used every day, so its layout needs to work first. In this compact Amsterdam room, the glass shower screen and vanity keep the different functions together without visually dividing the space. It is a useful starting point when you need to shower, wash and store essentials within a small floor area.

02

Storage and light where they matter

A vanity provides storage close to where it is needed. For a similar renovation, discuss basin height, space around the tap and mirror lighting as well as appearance. Here, the timber cabinet, black tap and blue tiles give this everyday space a distinctive character.

03

An easy-to-use separate toilet

The separate toilet shares the bathroom’s dark mosaic palette but has its own compact arrangement with a wall-hung toilet and hand basin. A clear floor makes cleaning underneath easier. Choosing fittings therefore means considering access, movement and cleaning as well as the model itself.
