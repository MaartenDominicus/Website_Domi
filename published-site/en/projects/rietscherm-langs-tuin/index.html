Project story

A closer look at the project

01

The result in everyday use

A comfortable seating area also depends on its surroundings. Here, a reed screen follows the garden boundary. Existing posts and wire fencing remain part of the structure, with the screen forming the visible finish on the garden side.

02

Choices for your space

For a similar job, we discuss how much screening you want and how open the boundary should remain. Height and material work together to shape its appearance. Existing fixing points also need to suit the screen being considered.

03

Finishing and maintenance

A natural screen changes with weather and use. Alongside its initial appearance, we consider maintenance and access beside the planting. A small adjustment can bring the seating area together without redesigning the whole garden.
