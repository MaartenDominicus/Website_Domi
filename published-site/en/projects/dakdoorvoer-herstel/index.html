Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Investigating the cause

When moisture is suspected around a roof penetration, both the junction and its surroundings need consideration. Water can travel away from the point where it enters before becoming visible inside. Describe when you notice moisture and any earlier repairs that have been carried out.

02

Repairing one junction detail

The repair shown concerns the junction around a single penetration. The photograph shows sealing material applied around that detail. It does not establish the condition of the entire roof; for a similar repair, the existing construction determines which approach is suitable.

03

A clear scope for a small job

Targeted maintenance starts with a clear scope: which detail needs investigation, what belongs to the repair and whether surrounding damage needs separate attention. Discuss access as well. You do not need to climb onto the roof for an initial enquiry; photographs from a safe position and a description help start the conversation.
