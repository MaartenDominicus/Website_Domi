Project story

A closer look at the project

01

More light while retaining character

A large glazed opening brings the surroundings closer to daily life indoors. In a timber home, that opening also needs to fit the facade. Here, timber cladding and dark green window frames work together. For your own renovation, we look at what can stay and where repair or renewal is needed.

02

Care at the connections

Cladding, windows and roof edges form the exterior together. Their connections deserve as much attention as the visible timber. For a similar plan, we discuss how rainwater can drain, which parts remain accessible for maintenance and which finish suits the existing home. There is no need to commit to a timber species or window system before those choices are understood.

03

Repair what lies behind the cladding

Damaged timber was visible behind the old blue roof-edge cladding. Such a situation calls for investigation into the cause and extent of the damage first. Simply covering it with new boards does not solve that problem. Distinguishing repair from replacement helps define the work and retain sound existing parts.
