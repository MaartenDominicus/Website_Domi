Project story

A closer look at the project

01

The result in everyday use

Continuous flooring connects the areas used for walking, sitting and reaching the stairs. In this living space, timber continues alongside the open staircase. The warm floor tone sits beside the existing dark stairs and blue wall.

02

Choices for your space

For a similar floor, we first consider what the existing subfloor allows. Junctions at stairs, skirting and doors also deserve attention. This makes the floor part of the room while keeping routes practical for walking and moving furniture.

03

Finishing and maintenance

Timber needs a finish suited to its use and the maintenance you prefer. We discuss how the room will be used and the appearance you enjoy. A considered finish brings everyday use, cleaning and the character of the flooring together.
