Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Start with the setting

Maja’s Hideaway was built as a new timber retreat. A woodland retreat benefits from a layout that responds to its setting. In Lieren, the long glazed elevation brings tree views into the living area. Timber outside connects the building with its surroundings. Orientation is therefore an early choice for your own build: where do you want views, daylight and more shelter?

02

Open living with comfort in mind

The kitchen, dining table and seating area sit in sequence within one open space. Different activities can take place without giving each a separate room. Timber in the floor, ceiling and frames keeps the finish coherent. For your own plan, extensive glazing also calls for consideration of shading and ventilation: daylight and a comfortable indoor climate need to work together.

03

Consider privacy and maintenance

The separate bedroom also has a large window outside. This keeps the woodland close without making sleeping part of the shared living space. Timber cladding, junctions around doors and windows and the interior finishes form one whole. With timber construction, discuss maintenance and access to external timber so you understand what the chosen finish will need later.
