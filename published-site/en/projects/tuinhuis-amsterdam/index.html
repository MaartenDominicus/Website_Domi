Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Getting more from the garden space

A garden should offer somewhere to relax without every corner becoming storage. This garden room combines an open lounge with enclosed storage behind double doors. They share a roof but remain separate in use: a place to sit alongside room for items you prefer to keep out of sight.

02

A layout shaped around your use

Timber cladding, a low bench and a connection to the terrace make the lounge part of the garden. For your own garden room, start with its use: how much seating do you need, what must fit in storage and which route needs to remain clear? Those choices determine the balance between open and enclosed space.

03

Staying outside after sunset

Lighting makes the seating area useful after sunset too. The roof provides shelter from above while the open side maintains contact with the garden; it remains an outdoor space. For a similar build, consider lighting positions and the required power supply early so connections can be planned alongside the timberwork.
