Project story

A closer look at the project

01

A practical addition

Our maintenance work extends beyond houses and garden buildings. Painting was also carried out on this inland vessel. Its superstructure has a red finish and a black walkway alongside the windows. It is a different setting, where access and careful work around existing fittings matter.

02

Choices before starting

For painting work, we first look at the surface and agree what is included. Frames, windows, fittings and adjoining areas each need attention. For your maintenance project, we therefore make clear which parts will be treated and which existing components will remain.

03

Finishing and maintenance

A maintenance plan works best when its scope is clear from the start. It may involve one component or several adjoining surfaces. We discuss access, preparation and the desired appearance, so you know exactly what belongs to the agreed painting job.
