Project story

A closer look at the project

01

A practical addition

A garden can do more than provide a terrace and space for plants. A timber garden room and a separate storage shed can help keep the main house more usable too. Here, both timber structures sit alongside the existing buildings, without needing to share the same shape.

02

Choices before starting

For a similar project, we start with what you want to store or use. Doors need enough room to open and the route from the house should remain convenient. Windows, the view from indoors and space beside a boundary also influence the most practical arrangement.

03

Finishing and maintenance

Outdoor buildings need care at edges and junctions, especially where different materials meet. For your garden we discuss the construction and maintenance of the timber. That helps you choose between an additional garden room, a compact shed or a combination of both.
