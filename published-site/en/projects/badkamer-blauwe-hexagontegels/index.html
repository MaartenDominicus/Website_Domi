Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Making a small room work

In a small bathroom the layout needs to work as a whole. The shower, basin and toilet must remain usable without one element getting in the way of the others. For your own room, begin with the clear route through the space and the movements you make every day.

02

Planning the shower details early

The shower in this bathroom sits beside the window. That brings attention to the junction between tiles and frame and to the surfaces that need cleaning after use. These details work best when dimensions and connection positions are considered together before the finishing begins.

03

Character without visual clutter

A distinctive tile can give a room character without every surface competing for attention. Here blue hexagonal tiles sit alongside white areas and a light basin. The illuminated mirror adds useful light; in your own bathroom, lighting, ventilation and finishing belong in the same plan.
