Project story

A closer look at the project

01

An essential for everyday use

Working drainage is part of a building you can use without unnecessary inconvenience. This Apeldoorn project involves a new underground connection through a garden with trees. Unlike an empty building plot, the route and working space need to fit around existing buildings and the layout of the land.

02

Why the pipe route matters

The shortest route is not the only consideration for drainage. A fall, or gentle downward slope, helps wastewater flow through a gravity drain. Bends and connection points also matter. Suitable routing and construction depend on ground levels and the existing connection, which cannot be established from a floor plan alone.

03

Knowing where work will take place

Work here includes excavation, pipe sections, joints, bends and a branch beside the building. As an owner, it helps to know beforehand where digging will take place and which routes may be less accessible temporarily. An agreed route clarifies the connection and what the work means for using your land.
