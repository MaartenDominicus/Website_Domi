Project story

A closer look at the project

01

The result in everyday use

A basin is used so often that its details can be easy to overlook. The tap and the space around the bowl still need to work comfortably. Here, the spout and two controls stand separately on the basin within the existing tiled finish.

02

Choices for your space

When considering similar fittings, we look at the available space and existing connections. The position of the spout needs to suit the shape of the basin. Access underneath and room for everyday items also belong in the discussion.

03

Finishing and maintenance

The junction with the wall affects how straightforward the area is to clean. We therefore consider materials, controls and maintenance together. A small bathroom update can fit the existing room without requiring every element to be chosen again.
