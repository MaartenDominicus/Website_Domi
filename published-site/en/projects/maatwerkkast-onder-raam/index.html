Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Behind the doors

A separate table originally stood beneath this window with belongings underneath. The low cupboard turns the same spot into enclosed storage, with a surface above for everyday objects. Extra storage therefore does not have to mean a tall cupboard elsewhere in the room, and the space above this piece remains open.

02

Along the window

Shelves behind the doors divide the contents over several levels. For your own cupboard, a useful layout starts with what you need to store: small items need different arrangements from wide or tall objects. The doors also need room to open, so you can reach the contents without first moving other furniture.

03

Before the fitted cupboard

The window can open above the top. Fitted furniture needs to allow for this from the start, along with its junctions with existing walls. Choose height and depth together: enough storage below, a usable surface and space for the window to move. The timber finish brings the top and doors together as one piece.
