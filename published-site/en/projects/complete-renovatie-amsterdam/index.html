Project story

A closer look at the project

01

Getting more from an existing home

A renovation is about how you use the whole home, rather than one attractive room. The tall glazed extension brings daylight inside and connects this home with its garden. Stained glass retains an individual character. An existing house can feel more spacious without giving every room the same appearance.

02

A kitchen starts with how you use it

A kitchen is comfortable to use when its layout and connections work together. Installation here includes positioning and aligning the cabinets and the associated connections. For your own kitchen, settle appliance positions, water and electrical points before installation begins: later changes can mean reopening cabinetry or finished surfaces.

03

The sequence behind a neat finish

Flooring and sanitary work also form part of this renovation, including the mosaic floor in the toilet room. These tasks overlap: pipework needs access before new floor and wall finishes are closed. Discussing the sequence helps you understand which decisions are needed early and which parts of the home may have limited use during work.
