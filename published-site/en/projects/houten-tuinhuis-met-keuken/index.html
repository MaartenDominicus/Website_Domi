Project story

A closer look at the project

01

More space with a fitting extension

This garden building was later extended with a sleeping alcove. That adds another use alongside the existing space to sit and prepare something in the kitchen. The newer exterior photographs show the extension connected to the original timber building. For a similar plan, we discuss which addition would make your own space more useful.

02

Everyday facilities close at hand

The compact kitchenette allows coffee or a simple meal to be prepared without repeated trips back to the house. Open storage keeps everyday items close at hand. When planning a similar kitchen, we consider the amount of worktop space and which appliances will actually be useful. Water, drainage and power need attention early: the preferred position of the kitchen has to fit the available connections.

03

Finishes suited to your use

Timber interior finishes and the adjoining terrace give this space its character. The right finish also depends on how often you use the building and the maintenance you are comfortable with. We discuss practical choices alongside appearance: doors that open freely, room around the table and materials suited to daily use. The photographs show the same finished space both outside and inside.
