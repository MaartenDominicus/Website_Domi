Useful to know in advance

What should I include in my first enquiry?+

Describe the room, the work and the location. Overview photos and approximate measurements help explain the situation. Mention what you want to keep and any open questions; you do not need a complete building plan to start a conversation.

Will a site visit be needed?+

Photos are a useful starting point. The substrate, existing connections, measurements or access may need a closer look on site. We discuss what information is needed before the scope and proposal can be made specific.

What affects the price?+

The scope, existing condition, pipe routes, access and material choices all matter. Check which preparation, installation and finishing work the proposal covers, and which choices or unknowns remain open.

Can I choose my own materials?+

Discuss your preferences before ordering. Dimensions, connections, suitability and delivery times need to fit the work. Also agree who orders the materials and which items you will supply yourself.

Can I stay at home during the work?+

That depends on the rooms involved and the facilities that remain available. Discuss access, dust, noise and periods when water, electricity or bathroom facilities may be unavailable. This helps you account for the practical impact when making choices.

How do we agree a practical schedule?+
